## 📊 Launch Metrics & KPIs

### Acquisition Metrics
- **Website Traffic**: Target 10,000 monthly visitors by month 3
- **Conversion Rate**: Target 5% visitor-to-signup conversion
- **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)**: Target <$50 across all channels
- **Channel Performance**: Track ROI by marketing channel

### Engagement Metrics
- **User Activation**: % of users completing business profile
- **Feature Adoption**: % of users using advanced features
- **Session Duration**: Average time spent on platform
- **Grant Matches**: Average matches per user

### Revenue Metrics
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: Track growth rate
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Target >$300
- **Churn Rate**: Target <5% monthly churn
- **Upgrade Rate**: % of free users upgrading to paid

### Customer Satisfaction
- **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**: Target >50
- **Customer Support**: Response time <2 hours
- **User Reviews**: Maintain >4.5 stars across platforms
- **Success Rate**: % of users finding relevant grants
